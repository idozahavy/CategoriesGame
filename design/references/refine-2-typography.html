Category 2/6 — Typography

Fonts will be self-hosted in the app (bundled at build time — no external URLs at runtime). If a font below isn't installed on this machine, your browser shows the closest fallback from the same stack — sizes, weights and line-heights are exact either way. Note for multi-language support: Nunito covers Latin + Cyrillic + Hebrew; Quicksand and Baloo 2 do not cover Hebrew/Arabic and would fall back to a system font for RTL languages.
